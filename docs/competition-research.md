## Challenge and judging

[CONAE's announcement dated 28 October 2026](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/desafio-nasa-space-apps-challenge-2026-crea-soluciones-para-monitorear-incendios-con-datos) confirms the MODIS/VIIRS harmonization challenge, regional and historical investigation, and FIRMS plus CONAE resources. The [official challenge URL](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/modis-and-viirs-hot-spots/) returned 502. The [Athlone organizer's challenge copy](https://hackathlone.com/challenges/modis-and-viirs-hot-spots) supplies the fuller statement: a web calendar should help users examine historical burning patterns, unusual conditions, and critical periods in a chosen area despite different sensor characteristics.

This app directly addresses the geographic investigation, raw historical calendar, sensor comparison, and reproducible harmonization-method parts. It **does not yet deliver a validated harmonized region-month history, calibrated anomalies, or predictions**. Its scientific contribution is coverage-aware matching, historically available input inference, explicit holdouts and release gates, with the measured outcome that the simple seasonal baseline beats the complex candidates on the selected pilot. Do not sell a closed gate as a finished forecast.

The [2026 Ajmer Local Lead page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/ajmer/) lists impact, creativity, validity, relevance, and presentation. This is current organizer-published evidence, explicitly marked user-generated by the site. The central judging guide was unavailable; exact global weights and Craiova judging arrangements remain unverified. The deck responds to those five dimensions through a named user, navigable atlas, scoped benchmark, challenge-linked calendar, and a clear demonstration.
